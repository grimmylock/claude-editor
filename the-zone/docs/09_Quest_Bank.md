# Quest Bank

Every quest is tracked as a checklist in World State (file 00, Section 14). Zones and landmarks
here are canon (file 06). Rewards use the file 08 formula; roll the bands.

* **Standalone quests:** one-scene jobs; pick one matching the player's zone/tier.
* **NPC storyline arcs:** 3 stages per named NPC. Offer Stage 1 at the first real conversation;
  each later stage unlocks when the previous one is turned in.
* Nothing fits? Improvise in the same checklist format, rooted in an existing landmark.

## Quest Rules (every quest)

1. **Arc stages are offered only by their own NPC, in person.** Write the giver in the quest block.
2. **Complication:** when a quest is accepted, the player rolls 1d6; write it in the quest block.
   The GM picks when it happens, but it must happen before the quest ends.

   |d6|Complication|
   |-|-|
   |1|None — a clean job|
   |2|**Rival:** another Stalker or squad is after the same objective and shows up|
   |3|**Not as described:** tougher target, a second target, or the item isn't what was promised|
   |4|**Double-cross:** the giver tries to underpay or ambush the player at turn-in|
   |5|**Clock:** done within 2 Days or the reward is halved|
   |6|**Better offer:** another faction offers more to hand the objective to them instead|

3. **At least two ways to finish**, moving different factions' reputation (e.g. deliver to the
   Contractors +1, or sell to the Ecologists +1 Ecologist / −1 Contractors). Name both up front.
4. **One non-Ru reward per quest:** +1 reputation, a permanent 10% discount at the giver's shop, a
   true rumor (file 10), or a free technician upgrade (file 08, Section 5).
5. **Loose ends first.** If the player carries an unused keycard, an undelivered drive or an
   unfinished arc stage, offer that thread before generic filler (T4-05, T5-04...).

## Part 3 — Main Story: The Heart of the Zone

The campaign's spine and its endgame. The three Secret Labs (Zone Map file, Hidden Sub-Nodes) each
hold one **Fragment**. All three together open **The Last Gate** in The Reactor Core. Track it in
World State as `Main Story: Act [n] — Fragments: Alpha ☐ · Beta ☐ · Gamma ☐`.

**The truth (GM only, reveal it piece by piece):** before the Zone existed, a program called
**Project Hearth** was studying something found under the reactor, which its staff called **the
Source**. The "accident" was an experiment that went wrong. The labs bred mutants such as the Dread
Stalker as guardians for the Source. The Antenna Grid broadcasts the Source's signal, and Obelisk
worships it as a god.

**Fragments are keys, not loot.** No trader buys them (Economy file). Losing one means getting it
back from whoever took it.

|Act|Where|Objectives|Reveals|Reward|
|-|-|-|-|-|
|1 — Origin|Secret Lab Alpha (Industrial Basin)|[ ] Open Sample Storage's sealed terminal with the Alpha Access Keycard [ ] Recover **Fragment Alpha** (the Origin Log)|Project Hearth existed before the accident; the accident was an experiment|Fragment Alpha; Major-quest reward crate (Loot Tables file)|
|2 — Guardians|Secret Lab Beta (Ghost Town), then Dried Lake Sub-Station|[ ] Recover **Fragment Beta** (the Lab Beta Research Drive) [ ] Have it decoded: Dr. Osei, or an Intelligence DC 18 check at any lab terminal|The mutants were bred to guard the Source; the drive names the Antenna Grid as the relay|Fragment Beta; +1 Ecologist if Osei decodes it|
|3 — The Signal|Secret Lab Gamma (The Antenna Grid)|[ ] Get into Lab Gamma: Brother Anatoly's arc (Obelisk Friendly) or find it yourself (Perception DC 18) and fight through [ ] Open the Sealed Vault and take **Fragment Gamma**|What the array broadcasts: the Source's voice, and why Obelisk serves it|Fragment Gamma; **The Compass** (Tier 6 artifact) in the Sealed Vault|
|4 — Allegiance|Abandoned City / The Siege Ward|[ ] Choose a **sponsor**: any faction at +3 (Friendly), or go alone [ ] Get through The Siege Ward with them|Each faction says what it would do with the Source|Sponsor's escort through the Siege Ward; going alone means no help but no debts|
|5 — The Last Gate|The Reactor Core|[ ] Fight through the Containment Hall and Control Room (T7-01) [ ] Open The Last Gate with all three Fragments [ ] Decide the Source's fate|Everything|**The Black Star** (Tier 6 artifact) behind the Gate; Legend rank|

T7-01 and T7-02 in Part 1 are Act 5. Dr. Osei's own arc (Part 2) is a separate mystery, but she's
the natural person to decode Fragment Beta.

### After the ending

The campaign doesn't stop at the Gate. Apply the ending's world changes, then keep playing:

- **Named mutants:** one per Tier 5-7 zone, given a name by the GM. A Named mutant has ×1.5 HP,
  +2 to attack rolls, and one extra trait borrowed from another mutant (e.g. a Dread Stalker that
  can Cloak). Drops 2 Mutant Parts plus one Tier 5 loot roll. Log each in Named NPCs.
- **Legend bounties:** a Legend-rank NPC per faction (Elimination, Target: NPC Legend), each with a
  name, a grudge and a Tier 5 weapon guaranteed in their drop.
- **Faction war:** the Faction Goals in `10_Living_World.md` keep running, and now you can tip
  every one of them.
- **New Game+ (optional):** a new character in the same world, keeping the ending's world changes
  and any Named NPCs still alive.
