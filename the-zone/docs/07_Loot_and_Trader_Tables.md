# Loot & Trader Tables (Layer 2 — Rolled Stock)

Used for trader rolled stock, NPC extra loot, containers and filler quest crates. Never for
baseline items (file 05). **One d100 = one item:** the roll gives a category; take the matching
item from the tier's pool below (or the named item) and price it within the band. Armor pieces
each have their own band.

Roll on the table of the **zone's or trader's Tier**, not the player's rank.

## How many rolls?

|Source|Rolls|
|-|-|
|Wandering trader|4-6|
|Established trader|8-12, restocked each new Day (file 10)|
|Defeated human NPC|File 08, Section 2 (their equipped weapon and armor are just taken)|
|Footlocker / stash / container|3-6|
|Quest crate — major/story quest (arc finale, Main Story act)|GM picks 1-3 fitting rewards, no roll. The way to hand out a guaranteed Tier 5 weapon.|
|Quest crate — filler quest|Roll normally on the zone's table|

**Rerolls:** an item the trader already has → stack it or reroll once. A category with no pool at
that tier → reroll on the same table.

## Modifiers

**Tier Drift** (containers and NPC loot only, not traders): for each item, roll d20 first; on 1-5
use the table one tier lower (minimum Tier 1).

**Tier 5 weapons are loot-only and rare.** A trader roll that lands on a Tier 5 weapon becomes
Junk. A container or NPC roll that lands on one: d20, 1-17 = that category's Tier 4 item instead,
18-20 = the Tier 5 item. Tier 5 armor and artifacts aren't affected.
