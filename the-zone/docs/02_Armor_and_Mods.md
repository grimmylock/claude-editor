# Armor & Armor Mods

Armor gives **Phys-DR** (vs bullets and melee) and **Anom-DR** (vs anomaly and psi damage), plus
Belt Slots (artifacts), Mod Slots and base MP. **Each DR is capped at 75%** after armor, mods,
perks and artifacts are added up. Prices are in file 07.
