# Living World — Days, Zone Events, Faction Goals & Rumors

The Zone changes whether or not the player acts. **Each new Day = one d20 Zone Event + one d6
Faction Goal roll.**

---

## 1. Days

The World State block tracks `Day: [n]`. A new Day starts when:
* the player sleeps or takes a full rest (Safe Zone, outpost, cot, campfire), or
* the player makes 3 zone crossings without resting (time passes on the road).

**At the start of every new Day, in this order:**
1. Player rolls **1d20** on the Zone Event table (Section 2).
2. Player rolls **1d6** on the Faction Goal table (Section 3) and the GM fills that box.
3. Established traders restock (file 07).
4. Print the full sheet + World State.

---

## 2. Zone Events (1d20, once per Day)

|d20|Event|What happens|
|-|-|-|
|1-8|Quiet day|Nothing big. The next hub or outpost the player visits has 1 extra rumor.|
|9-10|Caravan|A wandering trader is waiting at the nearest outpost or hub today (file 06), with 2 extra item rolls.|
|11-12|Mutant migration|Pick a zone next to the player's: its hazard chance is +15% for 2 Days. Mention it in a rumor or on the radio.|
|13-14|Faction push|Fill one extra box on a Faction Goal (roll the d6 table again).|
|15-16|Named NPC|Someone from the Named NPCs list makes contact: asks for help, sends a warning, or (a fled or let-go enemy) comes back with a grudge.|
|17-18|Anomaly shift|The anomalies in the player's current zone move: anomalies already searched can be searched for artifacts once more.|
|19-20|**Emission**|See below.|

**Emission.** The sky turns red and sirens sound. The player must reach shelter: any building
interior, hidden sub-node, outpost or Safe Zone. They have **3 tactical rounds**, or until the end
of the current zone crossing if travelling.
* Caught outside: **60 anomalous damage** (Anom-DR applies) and Irradiated.
* Afterwards, every anomaly in that zone can be searched for an artifact again, at **+3** to the
search roll. Emissions are the main way artifacts come back into the world.

---

## 3. Faction Goals

Each faction below is working toward a goal, shown in World State as a 6-box track. When a track
fills, its result happens **permanently**: log it under Improvised World Details, then give that
faction a new goal (GM writes one in the same style).

**Daily roll (1d6): fill one box on that goal.**

|d6|Faction|Goal|When the track fills|
|-|-|-|-|
|1|Obelisk|Seize The Terraces (Quarry Basin)|The Contractor outpost at Quarry Basin falls. Obelisk patrols Quarry Basin and the Ship Graveyard; Iryna's arc ends unless already finished.|
|2|Contractors|Fortify Ghost Town|Ghost Town's hazard drops by 1. The Old Bank becomes a full Tier 4 outpost. Raiders leave Ghost Town.|
|3|Raiders|Hold the Chokepoint Bridge (Black Valley)|Crossing into or out of Black Valley costs 200 Ru or a fight. Black Valley's hazard rises by 1.|
|4|Sentinels vs Liberators|Hold Vanguard Barracks|Two tracks racing; roll a d6 again, 1-3 Sentinels, 4-6 Liberators. The first to fill owns the Command Post for good (Sergeant Kade's arc follows the winner).|
|5|Garrison|Break the Siege Ward|The Siege Ward's hazard drops by 1, and Garrison escorts through it become available (Main Story, Act 4).|
|6|Ecologist|Map the Anomalous Epicenter|Artifact searches in the Epicenter get +2. Ecologist outposts sell an Anomaly Map for any zone (500 Ru: reveals all anomalies there).|

**The player moves the tracks too.** A quest done for a faction fills 1 box on its goal. A quest
against it, or killing several of its members, empties 1 box. Say so at quest turn-in.

---

## 4. Rumors

When the player visits a hub, outpost or tavern (or buys someone a drink), give **1d3 rumors**.
Draw each from something real: a Faction Goal close to filling, an unfound hidden sub-node, a
Named NPC's whereabouts, an unfinished quest thread, or a fresh Emission zone.

Rumors can be wrong. **Don't decide that in advance.** When the player acts on a rumor, they roll
1d6: **1-4 true, 5-6 false or badly exaggerated.**

---

## 5. Named NPCs

Add an NPC to the Named NPCs line in World State when they give the player a quest, trade with
them more than once, surrender or are let go, or flee with a grudge. One line each:
`Name — where — attitude — alive/dead/fled`. Keep it to about 10; drop dead NPCs once their story
is over. The Named NPC Zone Event (15-16) and the rumors draw on this list.
